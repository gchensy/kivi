* ``kivi.lifetime.conditional_matrix`` shifts a transition matrix to the state of the economy in the one-factor model of Belkin, Suchower and Forest (1998): defaults, downgrades and upgrades all move with one systematic factor, and the default column is the conditional PD of ``kivi.capital.conditional_pd``. The default state stays absorbing, so the shifted matrices of a scenario multiply into its cumulative PDs.
* ``kivi.lifetime.matrix_factor`` estimates the factor of a period from its count matrix by maximum likelihood, and ``kivi.lifetime.matrix_correlation`` the correlation under which the factors of several periods have a sample variance of 1.
* ``kivi.datasets.make_loan_panel`` takes ``correlation`` and ``factors``, which move the payments of all loans with a monthly systematic factor. The default, a correlation of 0, gives the same panel as earlier releases.
