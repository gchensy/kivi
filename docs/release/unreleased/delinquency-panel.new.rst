* ``kivi.vintage.delinquency_panel`` builds the monthly loan panel that ``vintage_table``, ``transition_matrix`` and ``roll_rates`` take from the instalment schedules and the payments of the loans. It applies the payments of each loan to its oldest unpaid instalment first, so that an amount paid early counts towards the next instalment, and at every month end up to ``end`` counts only the payments made by then. Each row gives the days past due since the oldest unpaid instalment fell due, the status in the buckets of ``make_loan_panel``, the amounts past due and outstanding, and with ``principal`` the unpaid principal. ``tolerance`` lets an instalment count as paid when a small amount of it remains unpaid. A loan leaves the panel once it is repaid, and keeps rolling after its last due date while it is not.
