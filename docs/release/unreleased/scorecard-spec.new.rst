* ``kivi.scorecard.ScorecardSpec`` keeps a whole scorecard for production: the ``BinningSpec`` of its characteristics, the points of every group, possibly rounded or adjusted by hand, the scaling, the points of groups that development did not have, and optionally a calibration of the PD and a master scale. The calibration is a shift or a Platt scaling of the log odds, or an isotonic calibration of the score. ``points``, ``score``, ``probability``, ``grade`` and ``reason_codes`` apply the scorecard to any sample, and ``score`` adds the points column by column, so applicants in the same groups get exactly the same score on every computer.
* ``ScorecardSpec.from_model`` builds the specification from the coefficients of a logistic regression on the WOE values, and ``to_json`` and ``from_json`` save and load it as versioned JSON, format version 1. Reading rejects repeated keys and ``NaN`` or ``Infinity`` in the text.
