* The new module ``kivi.reject_inference`` infers outcomes for rejected applicants, so that a scorecard developed on approved applicants can be refitted on all of them. ``fuzzy_augmentation`` splits each reject into a bad and a good row weighted by its PD, ``parceling`` labels the rejects of each score band at the band's bad rate with a seeded generator, or returns the expected weights, and ``reweighting`` weights the approved applicants of each band by the inverse of its approval rate. A ``factor`` raises the inferred bad rate, and bands without approved applicants raise an error.
