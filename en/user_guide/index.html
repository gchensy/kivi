User guide#

The user guide follows the steps of a credit risk model in the order of a project: compute features of the applicants from their transactions and repayment histories, profile and bin the candidate variables, encode them with WOE, select the variables and fit the model, infer the outcomes of rejected applicants, scale the model to a scorecard, or weigh the factors of an expert scorecard where there are too few defaults to fit one, evaluate and calibrate it, estimate the loss given default and the exposure at default of its loans, set the cut-offs and the policy rules, save the binning for production, monitor the model period by period, validate it as supervisors ask of internal rating models, compute the regulatory capital that its PDs require, follow the loans that it approves from their repayment schedules, and estimate their lifetime PDs and expected credit loss under IFRS 9. Charts draws the tables of every step. Risk modeling workflow runs the steps from the profile to the stability report on the bundled data, leaving out the features of event data and reject inference, and the Tutorial follows a whole project from the first applications to the loss allowance.

kivi keeps fitting and applying apart. Bin edges, category groupings, WOE tables, master scales and cut-offs are fitted on the development sample and then applied unchanged to every later sample, so that counts and rates stay comparable. The tables take the groups as they are, so category labels can be passed directly.

Every function follows the rules of Conventions, such as the order of its arguments and the meaning of weights, and the Glossary defines the terms that these guides use.

Risk modeling workflow

An end-to-end example: profile and screen the variables, bin and encode the shortlist, fit and scale a scorecard, evaluate and calibrate it, set a cut-off and report stability.

Risk modeling workflow
Time-window features

Point-in-time features of transactions and other events over windows of months or days, repayment behaviour from monthly status panels, and feature specifications saved as versioned JSON.

Time-window features
Univariate profile

One row per candidate: missing values, distribution and shape, event rates, the IV and chi-squared test of a quick binning, and the Gini and KS of numeric values.

Univariate profile
Binning

Quantile, uniform and custom edges, special values, merging fine bins, supervised binning by ChiMerge, decision trees, Best-KS and monotonic WOE, optimal binning under constraints on the share, the events and the WOE shape of every bin, the same binning of a continuous response such as an LGD, grouping the categories of categorical variables, and missing values.

Binning
Weight of evidence and information value

Definitions, the smoothing rule for empty cells, how to read the result, the trend of the WOE over the bins, how to encode new data, and the information value of supervised bins on a validation sample or by a permutation test.

Weight of evidence and information value
Variable selection

Correlation filtering by Pearson or Spearman correlation, variance inflation factors and their elimination, logistic regression, stepwise selection with positive coefficients, and the selection path of nested models on a validation sample.

Variable selection
Reject inference

Fuzzy augmentation, parceling and reweighting, to refit a model developed on approved applicants on all applicants.

Reject inference
Scorecard

PDO scaling, points per group, scores for new data and reason codes.

Scorecard
Expert scorecards

Weigh the factors of a judgment-based scorecard with the analytic hierarchy process, check the consistency of the comparisons and combine factor and sub-factor weights.

Expert scorecards
Model performance

AUC, Gini, KS, gains and ROC tables, with the score oriented so that higher means riskier, the confidence interval and DeLong test of an AUC, the generalized AUC of ordered outcomes such as LGDs, and the expected maximum profit of a credit score.

Model performance
Calibration

Prior correction, master scales built from PDs or fitted to default rates, tests of each grade’s PD and of single PDs, the Brier decomposition, grade concentration, back-tests of LGDs and CCFs, Platt and isotonic recalibration, and calibration to a central tendency by a shift of the log odds or by quasi moment matching.

Calibration
LGD and EAD

Realized LGDs from the cash flows of workouts with per-loan discount rates, realized CCFs of credit lines, LGD tables by segment and by bands of a driver fitted to the LGDs, the fractional logit, and the rules of Basel, the EBA and China side by side.

LGD and EAD
Credit strategy

Approval and bad rates of each cut-off, its revenue and profit, the confusion matrix, precision, recall and F1 of a cut-off, swap sets between two scores, and expected loss by grade.

Credit strategy
Policy rules

Hits, event rates, lift and unique hits of each policy rule, the overlap of two rules, the decision waterfall of a rule set with the book it leaves approved, and a search of the numeric variables for single-threshold rules.

Policy rules
Binning specifications

Bin a whole data frame at once, including category groupings, and save the edges and WOE tables as versioned JSON for production scoring.

Binning specifications
Population stability index

Compare the distribution of two samples, including categories seen in only one of them, and measure each variable’s effect on the score with CSI.

Population stability index
Model monitoring

A per-period report of the population, the known outcomes, AUC, Gini, KS and PSI of a score, and the PSI of every variable by period.

Model monitoring
Model validation

The tests of the ECB’s validation report for PD, LGD and CCF models: back-tests, the current against the initial AUC and gAUC, migrations between grades and their concentration.

Model validation
Regulatory capital

The IRB risk-weight functions of the Basel framework and of China’s 2023 rules side by side, stressed PDs and the systematic factor of the Vasicek model, and most prudent PDs for portfolios with few defaults.

Regulatory capital
Vintage and roll-rate analysis

Monthly delinquency panels from repayment schedules, cumulative bad rates by cohort and month on book, one-year default rates by start month and their long-run average, roll rates and transition matrices between delinquency buckets, and the bandwidth and tests of rating migrations.

Vintage and roll-rate analysis
Lifetime PD and expected credit loss

PD term structures from vintage curves, transition matrices and survival tables with early repayments as a competing risk, 12-month and lifetime expected credit loss over economic scenarios, and IFRS 9 stages.

Lifetime PD and expected credit loss
Charts

Draw WOE tables, ROC, precision-recall and gains curves, the distributions of events and non-events, calibration, cut-offs and their profit, PSI, monitoring reports, vintage and survival curves, transition matrices, the decision waterfall of a rule set and LGDs by group with matplotlib.

Charts
Example data

The bundled bank marketing data, a synthetic loan panel for portfolio analysis, synthetic applications for reject inference and the account transactions before them, and synthetic defaulted loans with the cash flows of their workouts.
